What is simulated

  • Page breaks come from a rough sketch of the rules at 54 lines a page, not the real layout engine; (MORE) and (CONT'D) are placed by it.
  • The page is not editable: click a paragraph to put the cursor there, then change its element with the toolbar or the number keys 1–7 and 0. Browsers keep Ctrl+1–9 for tabs; the app will use Ctrl as Final Draft does.
  • Rename and Delete in the draft menu do nothing.

Running it

From the project root: python3 -m http.server -d docs/prototype 8765, then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/. Opening the files directly also works in Firefox; Chrome may refuse the bundled fonts from a local file.
