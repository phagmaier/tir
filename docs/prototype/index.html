Tir prototypes

The brief governs behaviour; the chosen prototypes are visual references. Demo behaviour and rejected variants are historical. The data and backup states are simulated; yellow controls are for the demos, not part of the app.

Approved visual references

5 · The writing window, finished look · approved

Desk in Ink & Brass, with the paper switch in the sidebar footer (dark paper or white). New here: the Title page row at the top of the sidebar opens a form with a live preview, and the saved/backed-up indicator opens the backup panel, including the one-time GitHub connection.

Dark paperThe default. White paperThe light theme.

6 · Projects · List chosen

Projects, Import and the import report. “Empty library” in the yellow bar shows first run. The Delete dialog illustrates a fully backed-up project; see the brief for other backup states.

ListChosen. One row per project: title, active draft, pages, drafts, last edit, and the folder when two projects share a name. ShelfNot chosen. Title-page thumbnails, like scripts on a shelf.
